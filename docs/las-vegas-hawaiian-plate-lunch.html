The Ninth Island

So many Hawaii residents visit Las Vegas, and so many have moved here, that the city is nicknamed the Ninth Island, and downtown's California Hotel has courted island visitors for decades. A plate lunch here is the same as at home: rice, macaroni salad and a main.
