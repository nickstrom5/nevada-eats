What counts as an honor here

Two sources, both reported as facts rather than scores. One is the MICHELIN Guide American Southwest, which first covered Arizona, Nevada, New Mexico and Utah in August 2026, as MICHELIN lists it. The other is the James Beard Foundation: its Best Chef winners and America's Classics awards (2 places). Where an honor went to a chef, the line under the place says so. Reader polls, "best of" roundups and review-site stars don't count.

MICHELIN and the MICHELIN Guide are trademarks of Michelin. James Beard Foundation and James Beard Award are trademarks of the James Beard Foundation. Nevada Eats is not affiliated with either.
